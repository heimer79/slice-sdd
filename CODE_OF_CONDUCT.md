# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Slice to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good faith and welcome newcomers.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it clearly.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open (issues, Discussions, pull requests).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or spam.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through a private message on GitHub (@heimer79). Reports are handled with discretion.
